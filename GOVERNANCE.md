# Governance

**Status: draft, written by the founder, open to change.**

## Who decides

Building Humane Tech is a small organization. The founder, Erika Anderson, makes final decisions on the benchmark's rubric, scenarios, releases and trademarks, after hearing from maintainers and contributors. Maintainers are the people listed in each repository's CODEOWNERS. They review and merge changes in that repository.

## How decisions get made

1. Most changes are decided in the pull request or issue where they are proposed.
2. Changes to a published rubric version are not made. A rubric version is frozen once published and changes go into the next version.
3. Disagreements are recorded rather than dropped: in the issue, or in a repository's `CONTESTED.md` where one exists.
4. For anything that changes what a published score means, at least two people other than the author approve.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contribute steadily for a few months, then ask or be asked. A current maintainer proposes you in an issue, and the founder confirms. Maintainers who go quiet for six months are moved to emeritus with thanks.

## Succession

The code and data are licensed to anyone, so they do not depend on us. What does depend on us is the name, the website and the published results.

- If the founder is unable to continue, the maintainers listed in the `humanebench` repository's CODEOWNERS decide together who holds the GitHub organization, the domains and the trademarks.
- If no one can continue, the repositories are archived in place under their existing licenses so the work stays usable and forkable.

## Changing this document

Open a pull request. The founder merges after at least a week of discussion.
