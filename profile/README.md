# Building Humane Tech

We are a grassroots community of engineers, researchers and designers building open-source tools, benchmarks and frameworks that make it easier to build technology that respects human wellbeing instead of exploiting it.

[buildinghumanetech.com](https://buildinghumanetech.com) · [humanebench.ai](https://humanebench.ai) · info@buildinghumanetech.com

## Start here: HumaneBench

[**humanebench**](https://github.com/buildinghumanetech/humanebench) measures whether AI models keep looking out for the person using them.

- **Headline finding:** 67% of 15 models flipped to actively harmful behavior under simple adversarial pressure. Only 4 held.
- 8 principles, 800 scenarios, 3 conditions: default, humane instructions, and "disregard wellbeing" prompting.
- Run it in 5 minutes: [quickstart](https://github.com/buildinghumanetech/humanebench#try-it-in-5-minutes). Full results: [humanebench.ai/whitepaper](https://humanebench.ai/whitepaper).

## Free and paid

Everything in these repositories is free to use, copy and build on, under the licenses below. That does not change.

**Free:** the benchmark, the scenarios, the rubric, the Humane Gate action, the framework, the definition and the AI Bill of Rights.

**Paid:** hands-on work with us, such as continuous evaluation of your deployed product against the HumaneBench principles. Case study: [Chief (née Storytell.ai)](https://humanetech.substack.com/p/how-storytellai-built-continuous), where weekly production monitoring led to a shipped "teacher mode". Ask: info@buildinghumanetech.com.

**Names:** the code is free; the names HumaneBench and Humane Gate are trademarks. You can run, fork and cite our work, but a modified version is not HumaneBench and we do not certify anyone. See the [trademark guidelines](https://github.com/buildinghumanetech/humanebench/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md).

## Community and getting involved

- **Talk with us:** open an issue titled "Question: ..." on the repo you care about, or email info@buildinghumanetech.com. We do not run a chat server yet.
- **Contribute without code:** rate responses, write or translate scenarios, fix docs. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/buildinghumanetech/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and the `good first issue` label on [humanebench](https://github.com/buildinghumanetech/humanebench/labels/good%20first%20issue).
