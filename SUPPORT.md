# Getting help

- **A question about a project:** open an issue on that repository and title it "Question: ...".
- **A bug:** open an issue with the bug report template.
- **Security:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else, or privately:** info@buildinghumanetech.com.

We are a small team and aim to reply within 10 business days. A slow reply is not a no.
